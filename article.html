Do Trio Friendships Ever Work Out?

I could start off by saying that trios are toxic but I might have to spare that because maybe 0.01% chances they do. I’ve been in enough trio friendships to know that there’s something about being the third person that can make everything feel a little… awkward.

I’ve been in trio friendships since middle school, and somehow, I always end up wondering the same thing: do trio friendships actually work, or is someone always going to feel left out?

Because here’s the thing about trios: sometimes two people have a connection that feels different from the connection they have with you. And even if nobody is intentionally leaving you out, you can still feel like you’re standing slightly outside of the friendship.

Another thing is that one or more might even hate/dislike each other, but they stay together because the other person is also friends with them. Which makes me question: are trios even healthy?

At the time when I was in a trio friendship someone would always be left no matter how hard we tried. The other two could discussing something that one doesn’t know the context about. So the two talking could wonder “should we stop talking and try to include her”; at least that's what I would think in that situation. It really is hard to make everyone feel equal and included, and if they all feel included, it never really lasts.

My friends are talking about, “Are you in this group chat? Are you that one?” But I don’t even care anymore because if they made a group chat without me, why tell me?

The thing about me is I don't talk a lot in group chats, and I feel like I'm not the only one. You guys probably don't either. I do talk more in real life though. Okay back to the point, why can't I just be allowed to be me. If I'm not a person who talks a lot in group chats it shouldn't be an issue. It shouldn't be something that is talked about in the secret group chat.

And I think that’s another thing people don’t understand about trio friendships: being quiet doesn’t automatically mean someone doesn’t want to be included. The amount of times I would hear “are you ok” or “she doesn't really fuck with us like that”. But instead I’m actually having all these thoughts in my head about a conversation I had earlier or plans I'm making for the next week. Why can't one just be?

After being in so many trios I try to avoid them as much as I can. If my friend can't hang out with just me, I'm out. I'm not about to be left out the whole hangout as they talk about all these other people I don't know about.

I've always admired those people who are always with that one friend: how do they do it? I don't feel like I have someone that I can call my best friend and it's actually mutual.
