Not to Be Dramatic, But…

A place for the thoughts that don't always make it into the group chat.

Not to Be Dramatic, But… is a personal publication about growing up, friendships, school, relationships, opinions, and all the random thoughts that somehow turn into a full conversation in your head.

Some posts are serious. Some are funny. Some are probably a little dramatic. That's kind of the point.

This is a place to say the things you think but don't always say out loud.
